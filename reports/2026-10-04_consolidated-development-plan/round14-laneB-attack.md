- **F-S10's trigger and its text** (H-22, XC-CRED L497, S0.11 L673, S0.14 L703). Under requests 2.33.1 with urllib3 2.6.3 (the base Python 3.14.7, the same pair as JuniperCascor1) and requests 2.34.2 with urllib3 2.7.0 (JuniperCanopy1), a key with a leading blank, a trailing LF, CR or CRLF, an inner LF, or a leading blank and a trailing LF raises requests' `InvalidHeader`, a `RequestException` whose text holds "in header value" and the key, before any connect. A trailing blank raises nothing and relays nothing. So the data client's `RequestException` branch is the path, and the three refusal texts reach it. I tested my own hypothesis that a trailing LF alone passes requests' check and fails later in `http.client` (a `ValueError`, outside that branch and outside the three texts).
