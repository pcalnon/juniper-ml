## What the events show (the analyzer's reading)

- **307 feeder requests: 289 answered, 18 evicted, none left open.** In flight (W to end): median 2,817 ms,
  p90 3,633, max 4,203 — over the lane's 1 s period throughout.
- **13 watchdog fires in ~1,512 s (~31 an hour), every one FALSE.** At each fire the lane's CURRENT disabled
  episode was 0.3–2.0 s old, against the 30 s threshold, and a feeder request was in flight. The watchdog
  samples `disabled` on the 5 s slow lane and resets only when a sample sees it enabled; the feeder's cycle
  is ~4.9 s, so consecutive samples can all land in in-flight phases (aliasing). Lane B's model (Phase 9)
  predicted 37–44 false fires an hour at a 7 s cycle; Phase 9's v1 census saw none, with a detector that
  read the lane after the fire's own write.
- **3 of the 13 fires started eviction cascades: runs of 2, 11 and 4 lost responses, each ending by
  itself.** Each run's first evicted request was the one in flight at the fire. In the cascades, the
  responses of the in-flight requests would have landed 1.6–2.4 s after the fire; of the 10 fires not
  followed by an eviction, 9 had a response landing within 1.7 s and one 2.2 s.
- **The page-load gate write evicted the first request** (gate `True -> False` at 7.25 s, request 1 in
  flight), a run of 1. The synthetic app's mount cascade (every request evicted) did not reproduce live: it
  had a constant 3 s latency.
- **The scripted triggers take effect 1.3–3.4 s after they fire**, through the renderer's queue, so a
  trigger fired when a request is 1.5 s old mostly lands after that request ends. They did not test
  F-CANOPY-058; the watchdog's false fires did, 13 times.

## Instrument limits found by this run

- The fire record's third field (`__metricsStoreDisabledSince` before the dispatch) is always null: the
  watchdog's own function clears it before the renderer dispatches its `AddExecuted`.
- TOOK's horizons (1.0 s, 2.5 s) are shorter than the measured trigger latency (1.3–3.4 s), and the trigger
  fires too late in the request (≥ 1.5 s of a ~2.8 s median flight). A trigger fired at the request's ENTRY
  into `watched`, scored on "any re-enable while a request is in flight, whose response lands after the next
  request enters", would test the claim.
- T-tab's 1.0 s spacing is page time between two `evaluate` calls; it measured 1.9 s.
