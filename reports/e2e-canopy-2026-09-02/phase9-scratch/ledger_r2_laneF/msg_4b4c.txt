perf(idle): the replay weight drain's 2 Hz timer ran on every tab with nothing to drain -- it now waits for this page to start a replay, and the metrics panel's dead timer is removed

An idle dashboard's main thread is ~0.1% idle, and every dcc.Interval tick is a
store update that re-runs the page's selectors, so an idle timer's cost comes
straight out of response delivery. Two timers were half of the page's
steady-state ticks:

- replay-player-panel-weight-drain, a 2 Hz timer on no tab gate, drains the
  CAN-015g replay-weight ring buffer. Nothing fills that buffer today: cascor's
  replay frames carry no weights and canopy's metrics relay drops the key
  (F-CANOPY-057). The drain now ships disabled; a clientside gate, the only
  writer of its `disabled`, enables it once replay-player-session holds a
  snapshot_id. Against cascor a Stop does not clear the session
  (_merge_session overlays any non-empty response, F-CANOPY-056), so after a
  replay the drain runs until the page reloads, as it always ran before.
- metrics-panel-update-interval (1 Hz) had no consumer. Removed. Its period
  setting is still parsed and still drives nothing, and the two docs rows say so.

Parking the drain on an idle page cut the median response-delivery latency by
42% and 32% (two in-page A/B runs in opposite orders, juniper-ml
util/ad-hoc/2026-09-23_canopy_timer_park_ab.py). A live check of this build
against its parent scored the latency inconsistent (one cuts window beat both
neighbours, the other was 1.5-2.7% slower than both); an earlier check of the
same change on canopy#670's branch scored it consistent. Structure and the
session gate passed in both.

New src/tests/unit/frontend/test_idle_dispatch_cuts.py fails CI on any Interval
in the built app that no callback consumes, and pins the gate's wiring and its
rule (run under node). 7 of its 9 fail on the parent; the two premise pins hold
on both. Comments that claimed cascor emits weights, that stop clears the
session, and that WebSocket events write it are corrected.
