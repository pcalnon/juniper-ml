Init Noise Std:

Std of an optional seeded Gaussian perturbation added to the history block; 0 yields an exact deterministic init. THIS IS THE KNOB THAT DECIDES WHETHER ``seed`` HAS ANY EFFECT: the seed is consumed only inside ``if init_noise_std > 0``, so at 0 every seed produces byte-identical output. Defaulted from DEFAULT_MACKEY_GLASS_INIT_NOISE_STD (juniper-data#319) so a deployment can make this generator seed-sensitive without editing code.

Discard:

Transient steps to integrate and drop before collecting n_steps (settles onto the attractor).
