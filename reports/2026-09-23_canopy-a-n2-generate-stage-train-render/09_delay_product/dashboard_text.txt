Type:

Delay Product (sequence)

N Steps:

Length T of the raw series before windowing.

Lookback:

Window length L (number of steps per sequence window).

Horizon:

Forecast horizon h (the target is h steps after the window end).

Sample Dt:

Constant per-step elapsed time (the regular Δt), measured in the generator's time_unit.

Scaling:

identity

Advisory scaling reported in DatasetMeta: 'identity' (raw, no transform) or 'standardize' (train-split mean/std/min/max of dt + target). The NPZ stays RAW either way.

Lag1:

First delay τ₁ in steps back from the window end (0 = the window-end step). Must be < lookback (the delayed step must lie inside the window).

Lag2:

Second delay τ₂ in steps back from the window end. Must be < lookback. May equal lag1 (yielding a squared term x(t−τ)², still a non-linear capacity target).

Jitter:

Sampling irregularity: per-step gap = sample_dt * U[1 - jitter, 1 + jitter]. 0 yields regular spacing; values toward 1 are highly irregular.

N Components:

Number K of superimposed sinusoids.

Noise Std:

Std of additive Gaussian observation noise; 0 yields the exact closed-form signal (and an exactly-known product target).
